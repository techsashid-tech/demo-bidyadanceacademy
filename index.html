Where Temple Sculptures Come Alive in Rhythm

"Odissi is not merely a dance; it is a visual prayer carved in stone and breathed into life through devotion."

Located near Savarmati Hospital, Mahanadivihar, Odisha, Bidya Odissi Dance Academy stands as a dedicated sanctuary for the preservation, rigorous training, and promotion of classical Odissi dance. Rooted in the ancient treatises of the Natya Shastra and Abhinaya Chandrika, our curriculum honours the sacred Mahari and Gotipua traditions of Kalinga.

Editable Guru Profile • Classical Odissi Faculty

Mahanadivihar, Odisha

At Bidya Odissi Dance Academy, our Guru leads every student through a disciplined, compassionate, and deeply enriching artistic journey. Emphasizing the pristine grammar of traditional Odissi, classes integrate body conditioning, Mardala rhythm theory (Ukuta), Sanskrit & Odia poetic interpretation, and stage craft.

Teaching Philosophy

Every student is trained to internalize rhythm (Laya) and devotion (Bhakti) so posture and expression flow naturally from within.

Cultural Contribution

Preparing the next generation of cultural ambassadors through classical recitals, lecture-demonstrations, and heritage festivals.
